### Pain Points by Pillar

**Trusted Data:**
- No reliable source of information on the quality of a venue's alcohol-free programme
- AI trip planners hallucinate venue names, NA menus, and details that don't exist
- Venue websites bury or completely omit their non-alcoholic offerings
- Existing rating systems (Michelin, TripAdvisor, Pinnacle) rate different axes entirely
- Reviews mentioning "mocktails" or "non-alcoholic" are scattered, unstructured, and unreliable

**Effortless Planning:**
- Planning a trip around not drinking requires checking dozens of sources and still guessing
- No tool combines venue discovery, scoring, maps, and trip planning in one place
- The cognitive load of researching NA options for every meal and evening is exhausting
- Group trips where some people drink and some don't require double the planning
- Trip plans built by general AI tools can't be trusted and require manual verification

**Confident Experience:**
- Arriving at a venue unsure whether you'll be handed sparkling water or a proper zero-proof cocktail
- Feeling like a second-class guest when the NA "programme" is an afterthought
- Fear of missing out on the "real" experience of a city's nightlife and dining culture
- Social awkwardness when your drink order signals something you'd rather not explain
- Not knowing whether a hotel's minibar, room service, or restaurant will have anything worth ordering
